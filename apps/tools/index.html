rullino tools

Each page does one job with your photos in this browser: they are never uploaded, there is no account and nothing to install. Every tool ends with the few lines that do the same in your own code with rullino, the open photo-development library they are built on.

Web-ready exportResize a whole set of photos to a long side, sharpen them for screens and save them as JPEG, WebP or PNG, without their location and camera data. Apply a look to a folderGive a whole folder of photos the same look, or an edit you made in the playground, with previews first, then export them all at once. LUT makerGrade a photo with a look and a few sliders and download it as a .cube file for DaVinci Resolve, Premiere, Final Cut, OBS or a phone video app.

More tools are on the way. The playground is the full workspace.
